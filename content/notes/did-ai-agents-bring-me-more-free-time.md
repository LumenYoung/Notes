## Did AI Agent bring me more free time?

There was a naive thought I possessed, which was basically that if I embrace an AI agent, then I don't have to worry about things. The agent will help me manage most of them, so I have more free time and can focus on the few things that matter the most.

This myth has been broken right now. It is fascinating how many additional things have come up after we found out that agents could help us do some new stuff. It seems that as your capability grows, the realm you want to gain access to also grows with your ambition. So it ends up being more things to be managed by you and your agent.

Even though many of them revolve around trying to abstract things away from my deliberate thinking, it is still something that consumes my attention, at least for now. For example, with documentation management, etc.,l there are still a lot of things you have to do manually and check, so they don't fully liberate you from the noise.

I guess it's an intermediate state, or I will eventually have to find a way to abstract even more at a higher level, so that I don't have to consider things like what we are experiencing now.
