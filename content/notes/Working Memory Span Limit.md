Modern Cognitive Psychology found  that we are easily remembering things within 4 units, this is like the number of items you can easily put into your working memory. Maintaining more than 4 chunks would require constant effort to remind yourself on the previous chunk in order to not lose it.

This feels pretty relatable to [[Publish/notes//Subitizing|subitizing]] in the sense that there are **special algorithm optimizations for the a small numbers of items** in human brain.
